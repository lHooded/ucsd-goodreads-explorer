Method

0 = off (all qualifying curators). 100 ≈ top 100 by curator weight (log-linear shrink). This is who votes — not a book-level penalty.

How many non-normie lit-poll books a curator must have rated ★≥4 (plus a mild rare-poll floor). 0 = off.

Share of ★≥4 hits on (deep-poll ∪ school-canon) that are non-normie. Also tightens the commercial anti-signal cap (com_share). 0 = off. Never a book-level penalty.

Mix of among-raters score (0) and full-cohort score (100, unread → 0). Linear in score space so equal slider travel ≈ equal score change. Bayesian m still guards tiny samples at the low end.

Weights for ★5 : ★4 : ★3 = q² : q : 1 (only top-half shelf ratings count).

Each kept rating contributes pct^x (x≥1). Higher x sharpens true loves vs merely-above-median marks.

Earliest catalogued edition year across all Goodreads editions in the dump (not only popular reprints). Still noisy for classics / translations.
